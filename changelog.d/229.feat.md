Added a five-minute lab (`examples/lab`, documented on the "Try it in five minutes" page): three `kubectl apply` commands start a throwaway Postgres, a demo app and the radar in any Kubernetes cluster, ship a deploy whose migration drops an index, and show the radar detecting it, naming the deploy and reporting the `Index Scan -> Seq Scan` plan change. It needs no database, ArgoCD, Slack or Helm of your own.
